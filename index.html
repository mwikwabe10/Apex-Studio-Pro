How Apex Studio Pro Works

Quick 4-step guide to generating and exporting documents.

1
Select Document & Layout: Choose whether you are creating an Invoice, Quotation, Receipt, Certificate, Proposal, or Delivery Note, and pick a professional design style.
2
Fill in Details & Line Items: Add your issuer information, client details (which activate the verification lock), and line items. The live preview updates instantly.
3
Configure Seals & Signatures: Use the master toggle to show or hide the signature block, corporate stamp box, official seal, and serial identifiers.
4
Pay & Download PDF: Complete the secure verification fee via Paystack to remove watermarks and download your high-resolution PDF.
